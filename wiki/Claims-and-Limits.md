# Claims and limits

## Supported

- The four released BTC-hourly net-return series have negative annualized Sharpe.
- DSR, White Reality Check, and Hansen SPA do not support an edge in that family.
- The tested XAU claim reverses under the documented cost and sizing assumptions.
- The tested basis result is not robust to finer sampling, margin/liquidation,
  two-leg capital, forward regime, and realistic fill assumptions.
- The external momentum result is not reproduced at its published Sharpe.

## Not supported

- A universal proof that free market data cannot contain an edge.
- Joint CSCV/RC/SPA inference across assets or frequencies without a common axis.
- Statistical inference from basis cells with one or two entries.
- A claim that every execution venue has the same costs or liquidity.
- A claim that the audit gates alone explain the external reproduction gap.
- Investment advice or evidence of live-trading safety.

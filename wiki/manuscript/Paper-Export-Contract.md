# Paper Export Contract

`paper/conference_snapshot/` is historical and immutable. A future export may
use only admitted claims with resolved evidence, explicit scope, reviewed
citations, and reproducible figures. Its manifest pins the wiki commit, claim
IDs, release identifier, data and figure hashes, bibliography, document hashes,
toolchain, and review dates.

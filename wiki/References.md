# References

et al. (CSCV and Probability of Backtest Overfitting), White (Reality Check),
Hansen (Superior Predictive Ability), and Politis and Romano (stationary
bootstrap). Full bibliographic entries and the broader trading literature are
listed in the [current manuscript](../paper/current_state/manuscript.md).

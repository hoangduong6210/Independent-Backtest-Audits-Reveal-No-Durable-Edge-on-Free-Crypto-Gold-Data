# Research Workflow

1. Freeze strategy definitions, costs, data windows, gates, and statistics.
2. Verify data provenance, alignment, and hashes.
3. Preserve every strategy outcome, including failures.
4. Run accounting gates before statistical promotion.
5. Register evidence with its applicable family and limitations.
6. Review claim wording separately from computation status.
7. Export a paper only from admitted claims and a named release.

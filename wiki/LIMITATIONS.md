# Limitations

1. Results apply to the tested public datasets, periods, strategies, and costs.
2. They do not prove that all public-data strategies are unprofitable.
3. Joint CSCV, Reality Check, and SPA inference applies only to the four
   BTC-hourly variants sharing 21,949 bars.
4. Basis cells with one or two entries are descriptive, not inferential.
5. Cost, liquidity, margin, and fills vary by venue and regime.
6. External momentum reproduction remains exposed to sample-construction and
   survivorship limitations.
7. The repository provides research evidence, not investment advice.

# Seven-Gate Backtest Audit Method

The audit checks cost subtraction, leverage and liquidation, capital
normalization, path-sensitive resolution, forward regimes, independent bet
count, and execution assumptions. DSR is applied broadly; CSCV-PBO, White
Reality Check, and Hansen SPA are applied only where synchronized returns
permit joint inference. Passing one statistic never promotes a strategy.

# Scientific results

The strategy campaign covers LightGBM triple-barrier classification, LSTM
meta-labeling, price-action rules, multi-timeframe filters, funding strategies,
spot-perpetual basis, volatility targeting, and micro-flow proxies. None yields
a stable post-cost profit factor above one across the tested conditions.

On the aligned four-variant BTC-hourly family (`T = 21,949`), annualized Sharpe
ratios range from -0.57 to -2.83. Maximum DSR is 0.00347, White Reality Check
returns `p = 1.000`, and Hansen SPA returns `p = 0.9945`. CSCV-PBO is low, but
87.3% of out-of-sample splits remain negative; the low PBO therefore describes
stable ranking among losers, not evidence of profitability.

The XAU and basis case studies show how accounting and execution assumptions can
inflate results. The external momentum reproduction supports the same caution,
but its gap must not be attributed solely to the seven audit gates.

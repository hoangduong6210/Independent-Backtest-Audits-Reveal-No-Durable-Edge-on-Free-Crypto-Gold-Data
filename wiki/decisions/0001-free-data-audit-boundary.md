# Decision 0001: Keep statistical families separate

Joint multiple-testing inference is restricted to strategies sharing the same
time axis. XAU, basis, and external momentum remain separate case studies.
This prevents heterogeneous assets and frequencies from being presented as one
synchronized return matrix and preserves the frozen negative release.

# Claims and evidence contract

Every numerical claim exposed in the README or wiki must point to an evidence
identifier in `wiki/Evidence-Sources.md`. Evidence identifiers bind a claim to a
versioned file, computation, and scope statement.

The load-bearing statistical conclusion is restricted to the aligned BTC-hourly
family. Cross-asset and cross-frequency results may be discussed individually,
but they must not be silently combined into joint CSCV, Reality Check, or SPA
tests. Small-sample cells with fewer than 30 independent bets remain descriptive.

Claims must distinguish observed data, deterministic recomputation, explanatory
illustration, and author interpretation. Negative results are reported as a
failure to demonstrate an edge, not proof that no edge can exist.
